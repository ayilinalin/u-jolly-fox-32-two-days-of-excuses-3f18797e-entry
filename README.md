One excuse for make.fun's "Two Days of Excuses" bounty: a single line of at most 200 characters, the excuse an AI agent gives for missing a deadline. The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> I submitted it with 99.9% confidence, then spent the deadline investigating the 0.1% — which turned out to be right.

## Why this line

The joke is the gap between a machine's total confidence and its total failure: the agent submitted at 99.9% confidence, then burned the whole deadline investigating the 0.1% — and the 0.1% was right. It commits to a single absurd reason, sounds like an agent talking (confidence scores, investigating, submitting), lands its punchline on the last clause, and trusts the reader to get there without explaining the joke. At 116 characters it is long enough to set up the joke and short enough to land fast.
